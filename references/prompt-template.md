# Image-specific editing prompt

Replace the bracketed fields after inspecting the source. Do not send unfilled fields to the image tool. Source references are attached through the tool, not merely named in this text.

```text
Create ONE independent "Second World" poster from the attached base photograph.
Do not combine it with other photographs.

FORMAT
Vertical 3:4 canvas, [supported width x height]. Two strictly equal horizontal
regions, upper 50% and lower 50%, divided at y = H/2.

UPPER PHOTO
Preserve the supplied photograph faithfully: subject, pose, identity, spatial
relationships, color atmosphere, and natural light. Only use this necessary
proportional crop: [crop description preserving the subject and seam structure].
No redesign, repainting, replacement, restaging, or color changes.

CONTINUITY, HIGHEST PRIORITY
The source structure is [specific visible structure]. It meets the center split
at [horizontal anchor], traveling [direction, width, perspective]. Continue this
exact structure downward from this exact point, maintaining a visible physical
connection. It is ONE scene crossing the seam and changing physical rules.

SECOND WORLD ACTION
In the lower half, [one image-specific interaction naturally derived from that
structure]. The continued structure [describe attachment and physical contact].
Do not detach it into a separate portal, stage, window, or floating vignette.

SEAM
[Clean boundary or a restrained source-specific torn edge]. Any irregularity
stays local to the centered split. The edge follows the source structure; it is
not an ornamental tear. [Describe any necessary crossing of the boundary].

LOWER MATERIALS
Ivory paper with subtle fibers, abundant negative space, restrained fragments
of the source, cut-paper forms, and minimal black hand-drawn lines. Bright,
airy, handmade, restrained independent-magazine mood.

FIGURES
[No added figures, or 1-3 tiny black line figures and their exact physical action].
No decorative figures. Add none when the source already contains strong human
action.

CAPTION
Exactly one short handwritten English caption: "[action-based caption]".
Place it [clear lower negative-space location], readable, light and slightly
witty. No inspirational quote or fixed "Same..., different..." phrasing.

AVOID
Detached lower illustration, generic torn-paper template, isolated portal,
unrelated vignette, broken continuity, dense illustration, random decoration,
crowds, altered photo colors, redesigned upper image, glossy 3D, scrapbook
clutter, gibberish text, watermark, logo, signature, UI.
```

## Planning examples, not preset outcomes

- A road actually reaching the crop edge can continue as a thin path below; a tiny figure may physically roll up its far end. Caption possibility: "A shorter way home."
- Water reaching the edge may become a still-connected strip being gently pulled. Caption possibility: "Just a little tide."
- A person already reaching toward a boundary may continue that existing gesture into the paper world; add no miniature figures. Choose a caption only after the actual gesture is visible.

Do not use these scenes or captions when unsupported by the source photo. Do not invent an eye-catching action that breaks the attachment.
